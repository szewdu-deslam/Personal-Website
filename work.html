What I do

My work

Writing, visual storytelling and communications.

My work brings together writing, photography and digital communications. These are the main areas I focus on and how I approach telling stories about people, science and technology.

Writing

Writing about research and ideas

I write about research, faculty and students at the University of Maryland’s Department of Computer Science. My stories make complex ideas accessible and highlight the people behind new discoveries.

People

Listening to people’s stories

People are at the center of my storytelling. Through interviews and profiles, I explore the experiences, motivations and ideas behind the work of researchers, educators and students.

Multimedia

Telling stories through images

My multimedia work includes photography and event coverage. I use images and captions to document experiences, bring stories into focus and help digital audiences connect with a subject.

Communications

Connecting university communities

I create web content, newsletters and social media for university communities. I also support communications for Computing Catalyst, helping students learn about opportunities in computing.
